north star
// north star

Memoir should feel less like an app and more like opening the night sky over your own days — celestial at rest, cartographic when you lean in.

One continuous gesture connects everything: the globe is the homepage, the map is the same surface zoomed in, and a single entry is just the moment you stopped to listen. Nothing else competes for attention.

02thesis/13
memoir
six decisions

Six calls, made.

The brief left six open decisions to the designer. Here is the position taken on each — the rest of the deck is what they look like in practice.

01hero view
"What does opening Memoir feel like in the first two seconds?"
A slow-rotating Earth at night. Three.js stays, but it lives on the same Mapbox v3 globe projection — scroll-zoom carries you continuously from sky to street. The path back to the globe is just ↑.
02browsing
"Spatial, temporal, or both?"
Three surfaces, one time brush. Sky (globe) for "all of it." Atlas (map + sparse spine sidebar) for "the walk I took in October." Roll (photo grid) for "that café I photographed last fall." One keystroke between them.
03detail view
"What's on screen besides the media itself?"
Compositions, not a single template. A photo is full-bleed with its session filmstrip; audio is a waveform with a reading transcript; moment is a cartographic crop with the song; note is purely typographic. Arrows flip neighbors (or frames within a session) with crossfade.
04density & pattern
"What does '47 times in this neighborhood' look like at a glance?"
At low zoom, dots dissolve into warm heatmap fog. Age cools the palette (recent = ember, old = ash). Photos are what give the map its density — the deliberate spine entries are the named landmarks within it.
05facets & filter
"Where do facets live?"
A quiet left-edge rail for everyday slicing; the ⌘K command palette for power queries (type:audio in:Lisbon during:autumn). The sidebar's default is grouped by day, chronological.
06tone
"How does this feel different from a productivity tool?"
Editorial — not dashboard. Instrument Serif over Geist over JetBrains Mono. Near-black warm ink, four luminous embers, breath-slow motion. UI fades after four seconds idle — only the map remains.

Deliberate.

The three types the owner chose to mark. Sparse, intentional — a few a day at most. Each one earns its own tile, its own full-screen detail view, its own line in the timeline.
audio
~2 / day
a stopped moment with a voice in it
moment
~3 / day
"I was here, this song was on, that was the weather"
note
~1 / day
a written thought, often after the fact
where
Atlas sidebar (chronological spine) · individual map dot · own detail view
find by
memory of when or where — "the audio I made walking home in October"
// 02 · the roll

Ambient.

Photos — dozens per day, often in bursts at one spot. Not peers to the spine. They live in their own surface, auto-grouped into sessions, and they feed the map's density without ever flooding the sidebar.
photo
~30 / day
a captured frame — often part of a burst at one place
grouped as
sessions — auto-clustered by time + place, like Google Photos. ~30 frames become 1 session tile.
where
Roll surface (grid view) · map dots (density) · "neighbors" rail of nearby spine entries
in the atlas
Sessions appear as one tile per burst, not one per frame
find by
scanning until your eye recognizes it — "that café I shot last fall"

motion · idle

How it breathes.

Memoir is mostly still. The motion that exists has a purpose: orient on arrival, get out of the way after, and announce the new with one quiet pulse.

On openopening shot
The globe rotates 4° over 1.2s, then rests. Stars hold a slow parallax (8s loop). The today bar fades in last.
1200ms
cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)
Zoom transitionsky → map
A single continuous transform. Three.js cross-dissolves into Mapbox at zoom 6, dots in-place. Both directions.
900ms
scroll · ⌘↑
Detail entryfull-screen flip
Selected entry expands from its map dot, surrounding chrome dims to 0.2. Neighbors fly in from below 150ms later.
600ms + 150ms
spring · stiff
Detail flip← →
Crossfade with 60px lateral drift. The next entry's metadata replaces piece by piece; media is a hard cut to avoid motion sickness.
400ms
opacity + transform
Idleafter 4s
Chrome fades to 0.15. Only the map & entries remain. Any mouse movement restores it in 250ms.
4s · 800ms
opacity only
Live arrivalnew from mobile
One ember pulse from the dot's location, 2.6s. Sidebar tile slides in from top. No sound, no toast.
2600ms pulse
once · then settle
Brush timelinefilter feedback
Map dots outside the brush dim to 0.15 over 250ms. Sidebar tiles fade their out-of-range siblings.

linear · cheap
